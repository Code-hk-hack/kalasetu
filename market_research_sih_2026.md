# SIH 2026 Market Research & Dataset Guide
**Project**: AI Business Manager for Marginalized Artisans

This document compiles the verified 2022–2025 market research data, government reports, and ML datasets required to defend your project in front of SIH judges. Use this data to build a **real, defensible pricing model** and prove your **Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy**.

> [!TIP]
> **Pitch Strategy Reminder:** Judges are tired of fake "black box AI". Use the Kaggle datasets below to train a simple, honest regression model for pricing, and use the official government census data to prove *why* a voice-first UX is mandatory.

## 2. Demographics & The "Why Voice-First?" Justification
*Use these official government stats to justify why traditional e-commerce apps fail for your target audience, and why your voice-first, low-literacy UX is the only solution.*

| Metric / Report | Source / Direct Link | Pitch Talking Point & Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **35.22 Lakh Handloom Workers** | [PIB Release (Ministry of Textiles)](https://pib.gov.in) | Quick official citation. Proves the massive Total Addressable Market (TAM) (26.73 lakh weavers, 8.48 lakh allied workers). |
| **25.46 Lakh Women Workers** | [PIB Women in Handloom Release](https://pib.gov.in) | Proves the gender dynamic. 72% of the market is women, requiring a UX tailored to rural female digital literacy levels. |

| **Pehchan Artisan Records** | [Data.gov.in - Pehchan Data](https://data.gov.in) | State-wise artisan registration data. Crucial for your onboarding strategy and identity verification. |
| **Handicraft Artisan Data** | [Dev Commissioner Handicrafts](http://handicrafts.nic.in/) | State-wise Pehchan records, artisan identity, and craft ecosystem details. |

## 4. Market Demand, ONDC, and Export Opportunities
*Where do the products go? Use these reports to prove that there is massive buyer demand via ONDC and international exports.*

| Market / Platform | Source / Direct Link | Pitch Talking Point & Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ONDC Opportunity Report** | [DPIIT - Democratic Digital Commerce](https://ondc.org/) | Use to explain open-network commerce. ONDC is a national priority—aligning your app as an ONDC Seller Node is a winning move. |

| **EPCH Export Data Page** | [EPCH Export Data](https://www.epch.in/) | Year-wise handicraft export figures. Verify the latest period before presenting. |
