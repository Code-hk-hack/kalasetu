# SIH26090 Pitch Strategy & Market Sizing (v3)
**Project**: AI-Driven Zero-Barrier Marketplace & Discovery Ecosystem for Marginalized Artisans
**Problem Statement ID**: SIH26090

This document compiles the core pitch narrative, Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy, market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM), and our expanded feature ecosystem.

> [!TIP]
> **Core Pitch Narrative (Hinglish):** "Amazon aur GoCoop jaise platforms rural artisans ke liye fail ho chuke hain kyunki wahan digital literacy chahiye, aur buyers ke liye fail hain kyunki authentic handmade aur factory-made machine copy mein koi farak nahi dikhta. Hum ek **Dual-Sided AI Ecosystem** bana rahe hain: Artisan sirf bolkar aur 10-second ki making video daalkar direct sell karega aur daily hisab-kitab sunega; jabki buyer Instagram reel ka screenshot daal kar matching handmade craft khoj sakta hai ya apne kamre ki photo daalkar AI home decor recommendations le sakta hai."

## 2. Expanded Feature Ecosystem (The Winning Differentiators)

### A. Buyer-Facing Discovery & Immersion
1. **AI Visual Search ("Reels-to-Artisan"):** Upload any screenshot from Instagram Reels/Pinterest $\rightarrow$ CLIP vector search finds authentic handmade matches in <200ms.
2. **AI Room Decor & Space Stager:** Upload an empty wall or room photo $\rightarrow$ Multimodal Gemini Vision analyzes wall color, lighting, and dimensions to suggest complementary handicrafts.
3. **Custom Commission Pipeline:** If an item isn't in stock, send a custom request with specs and escrow milestones directly to verified artisans.
4. **10-Second KalaReels Feed:** Authentic, unforgeable videos of the crafting process directly destroying buyer skepticism over counterfeit machine goods.
5. **Instant SMS & Geo-fenced Tracking:** Order confirmations sent via SMS; map tracking displays verified pickup within registered artisan clusters.

### B. Seller-Facing Micro-Enterprise Empowerment
1. **Voice Dukandar Daily Hisab-Kitab:** Spoken daily briefing in regional language detailing daily materials used, earnings, and completed vs. pending dispatches.
2. **Creator Micro-Sites & Profiles:** Dedicated shareable storefronts (`karigar.in/@artisan`) showcasing heritage, awards, GI tags, and direct checkout.
3. **AI Content Studio Assistant:** Auto-generates SEO descriptions, storytelling captions, and tags for short videos and photos.
