SLIDE 2: IDEA TITLE & COMPLETE SOLUTION ARCHITECTURE

Idea: An AI Virtual Business Manager + Zero-Barrier Marketplace

1. The Core Problem (The Last-Mile Digitization Gap)

  • For Rural Artisans (Supply Side Failure): 72% female with <30% digital literacy. Existing platforms (Amazon Karigar, GoCoop) require English fluency, professional studio equipment, tax paperwork, and complex cataloging forms. Middlemen extract 300% to 500% profit cuts.
  • For Urban Buyers (Demand Side Failure): Buyers see trending crafts on Instagram Reels/Pinterest but cannot find authentic rural makers, negotiate custom pieces, or verify genuine handcrafts from cheap factory machine fakes.

2. Our Complete Solution (Starting Core + Advanced Suite)

  • CORE FEATURE 1 AI Photo Studio (Offline Background Removal): Raw phone camera photo → On-device TFLite (U2Net) removes messy rural backgrounds and color-corrects to GeM/ONDC e-commerce standards with ₹0 cloud cost.
  • CORE FEATURE 2 Voice-to-Catalog Engine (Bhashini AI): Artisan speaks in their mother tongue → Bhashini transcribes and translates 22 Indian languages → LLM generates professional SEO titles, product stories, and specifications.
  • CORE FEATURE 3 Explainable Cost-Plus Pricing ML: Regression model trained on real Kaggle handmade datasets calculates a transparent, fair price (Raw Materials + Labor Hours + Market Average), preventing underpricing and building buyer trust.
  • CORE FEATURE 4 Instant Digital Storefront & ONDC Linkage: Automatic storefront generation with 1-click export to ONDC network, WhatsApp catalog, and direct-to-consumer QR links.
  • EXPANDED USP 1 "Reels-to-Artisan" Visual Search: Screenshot from Reels/Pinterest → MobileCLIP + Supabase vector search matches authentic inventory in <200ms.
  • EXPANDED USP 2 AI Room Decor Space Stager: Upload room/wall photo → Gemini Vision suggests matching handmade decor.
  • EXPANDED USP 3 10s KalaReels Authenticity Feed: Proof-of-making micro-videos destroying counterfeit machine fake doubts.
  • EXPANDED USP 4 Spoken Dukandar Hisab-Kitab: Voice-first daily business summary (material stock, revenue, orders).

SLIDE 3: TECHNICAL APPROACH & ZERO-COST ARCHITECTURE

End-to-End Technical Workflow (6-Step Pipeline)

  • 1. Raw Capture: Artisan snaps raw product photo, speaks voice note, or shoots 10s making video on basic Android phone.
  • 2. Edge Vision (₹0 Cloud Cost): On-device TFLite (U2Net) isolates product and wipes background locally without cloud server bills.
  • 3. Voice-to-Listing: Voice Credits (Sarvam/ElevenLabs) + Bhashini API transcribes regional audio → generates bilingual e-commerce listings.
  • 4. Dynamic Pricing Engine: Scikit-learn Cost-Plus ML Regression suggests fair retail and wholesale pricing.
  • 5. Supabase All-in-One Cloud Engine: Supabase (Free Tier) manages user authentication, relational order tables, product catalogs, photo/video bucket storage, and vector similarity search (`pgvector`).
  • 6. Logistics, Tracking & SMS: Instant order SMS via DLT Gateway (Fast2SMS/MSG91); live order tracking with Leaflet OSM Geo-Fencing around registered SFURTI clusters + ONDC Beckn Protocol.
