🛑 Section 1: The Critical Problem & Existing Market Loopholes

India’s traditional handicrafts and handlooms represent the country's second-largest employment sector after agriculture. Despite immense global demand, the rural artisans who preserve this heritage face systemic exclusion from the digital economy.

1. The "Retailer-Connection" Trap

Existing e-commerce giants (Amazon Karigar, Etsy, Flipkart Samarth) primarily connect buyers with urban retailers and aggregators. The rural creator remains anonymous, invisible, and dependent on exploitative middlemen who take 60%–70% margins.

KalaSetu's Direct Artisan Attribution

Zero-middleman direct linkage. Every product features an immutable digital Artisan Pehchan Card, direct QR contact, and personal audio lineage. Buyers connect directly with the maker.

2. The Digital Literacy & Form Barrier

Conventional seller portals require typing, complex SKU categorization, GST details, English descriptions, and high-spec desktop setups—impossible for rural artisans.

Voice-First Vernacular Cataloging

The artisan speaks in their local dialect for 10 seconds: "बगरू हैंड-ब्लॉक दुपट्टा, प्राकृतिक नील रंग, 2 दिन लगे, 1200 रुपये". KalaSetu’s AI extracts, structures, and creates luxury listings instantly.

3. Counterfeits & Unverifiable Claims

Industrial powerlooms and factory machine-prints sell cheap polyester as "Pure Handwoven Silk" or "Natural Indigo", destroying the market value of authentic handmade crafts.

ClaimSafe AI & Verifiable Product Passport

AI scans 16+ high-risk claims, enforces hard publishing gates, and issues a tamper-evident Product Passport verifying craft technique, materials, and GI heritage cluster.

4. The Financial Blindspot (Unclaimed Schemes)

Over ₹13,000 Cr in government support (PM Vishwakarma, Pehchan IDs, Mudra loans) remains unclaimed by rural artisans due to lack of awareness and complex paperwork.

Kala Funds Financial Aid Hub

An integrated scheme engine where artisans discover eligible welfare programs, toolkits, collateral-free credit, and NGO grants in 1 tap directly from their phone.

⚡ Section 2: Complete KalaSetu AI Master Feature Catalog

16 Verified Features
🎙️ 1. Voice-to-Catalog Smart AI
Multilingual speech recognition (14+ Indian dialects). Converts casual voice notes into structured product drafts with title, materials, dimensions, and craft classification.
🛡️ 2. ClaimSafe AI Verification
Scans 16+ sensitive claims (100% pure silk, GI Tagged, Natural dye). Hard publishing validation gate and public text sanitizer to prevent buyer misrepresentation.
⚖️ 3. FairPrice Guard & Wage Defense
Cost-plus formula calculator enforcing fair artisan hourly rates: Cost = Raw Materials + (Hours × Rate) + Overhead. Warns against ₹0 labor and predatory middleman discounts.
🛂 4. Verifiable Product Passport
QR-code enabled digital provenance certificate. Displays verified material claims, GI cluster attribution, artisan lineage story, and strict privacy consent defaults.
📸 5. Truthful Image Studio
Safe image enhancement policy (strictly non-synthetic, preserves authentic handmade imperfections). Live before/after toggle and real-time blur, underexposure, and glare diagnostics.
📝 6. Craft-Aware Missing Field Wizard
Contextual templates for Bagru Block Prints, Jaipur Blue Pottery, Bastar Dhokra, etc. Non-intrusive step-by-step wizard prompting for vital buyer specifications.
📶 7. Offline-First Drafts & Conflict Sync
IndexedDB v2 local storage with retry queue. Operates 100% offline in rural villages. Timestamp conflict management creates safe copies without silent overwrites.
🏛️ 8. Facilitator & NGO Review Desk
5-metric audit dashboard for cluster coordinators and SHG leaders. Queue for inspecting pending claims, vetting fair prices, and submitting audit field notes.
📍 9. Cluster Geo-Fencing Dispatch
Validates that product dispatch origin physically matches the recognized geographical heritage boundary (e.g. Bagru cluster polygon), proving authentic provenance.
🎥 10. Kala Reels ➔ Direct 1-Tap Buy
Short-form making videos showcasing raw artisanal process. Floating buy-now button allows consumers to discover, learn, and purchase directly inside the video flow.
🖼️ 11. AI Room Décor Simulator
Space Stager: Buyers upload a photo of their living room, empty wall, or corner table. AI visualizes how authentic vases, tapestries, or brass lamps fit their home.
🔍 12. AI Multi-Object Visual Search
Uploads a lifestyle or Pinterest image and detects multiple objects simultaneously (e.g., [Terracotta Vase] + [Jute Dhurrie]), matching each to KalaSetu catalog items.
💰 13. Kala Funds (Financial Aid Hub)
Direct discovery of government schemes: PM Vishwakarma (toolkit grants & ₹3L collateral-free loans), AHVY, Mudra loans, and Pehchan artisan card benefits.
📊 14. Artisan Productivity Score
Business health score (0–100) based on fulfilled orders, catalog updates, hisab ledger consistency, and buyer ratings. Enhances loan creditworthiness.
🤖 15. KalaSetu Saathi (Voice AI Companion)
Conversational agent assisting buyers in finding heritage gifts and assisting artisans with profit calculations and daily stock status in regional languages.
🎁 16. Patron & Artisan Reward System
Artisan Karma points for verified sustainable dyes; Heritage Patron badges for repeat buyers supporting rural craft clusters.
