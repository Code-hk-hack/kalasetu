🛑 Section 1: The Real Problem & Market Loopholes KalaSetu Solves

Root Cause Analysis

1. The "Retailer-Connection" Trap

Mainstream e-commerce platforms (Amazon Karigar, Etsy, Flipkart) link customers with urban aggregators and retail wholesalers. The actual village creator gets zero visibility, zero brand recognition, and only 25%–30% of final retail value.

KalaSetu's Direct Artisan Attribution

Zero-middleman direct linkage. Every product features an immutable digital Artisan Pehchan Card, direct QR contact, and personal audio lineage. Buyers connect directly with the maker.

2. The Digital Literacy & Form Barrier

Conventional seller portals require typing, complex SKU categorization, GST details, English descriptions, and high-spec desktop setups—impossible for rural artisans.

Voice-First Vernacular Cataloging

The artisan speaks in their local dialect for 10 seconds: "बगरू हैंड-ब्लॉक दुपट्टा, प्राकृतिक नील रंग, 2 दिन लगे, 1200 रुपये". KalaSetu’s AI extracts, structures, and creates luxury listings instantly.

3. Counterfeits & Unverifiable Claims

Industrial powerlooms and factory machine-prints sell cheap polyester as "Pure Handwoven Silk" or "Natural Indigo", destroying the market value of authentic handmade crafts.

ClaimSafe AI & Verifiable Product Passport

AI scans 16+ high-risk claims, enforces hard publishing gates, and issues a tamper-evident Product Passport verifying craft technique, materials, and GI heritage cluster.

4. The Financial Blindspot (Unclaimed Schemes)

Over ₹13,000 Cr in government support (PM Vishwakarma, Pehchan IDs, Mudra loans) remains unclaimed by rural artisans due to lack of awareness and complex paperwork.

Kala Funds Financial Aid Hub

An integrated scheme engine where artisans discover eligible welfare programs, toolkits, collateral-free credit, and NGO grants in 1 tap directly from their phone.
